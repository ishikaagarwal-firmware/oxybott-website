Our Mission

Democratizing Robotics & STEM Education

At Oxybott, we believe every child deserves the tools, confidence, and curiosity to build the future. We design intelligent, open-source educational hardware that turns abstract code into tangible kinetic creation.

Who We Are

Born from a Passion for Hands-On Engineering

Founded by roboticists, educators, and embedded hardware engineers, Oxybott was created to address a critical gap in modern STEM education: kits were either too simplistic toys that lacked real programmable depth, or complex industrial hardware that overwhelmed teachers and students.

By standardizing on the powerful 240MHz ESP32 dual-core architecture, we brought real Wi-Fi and Bluetooth IoT capabilities to students at accessible prices, supported by our anti-reverse Easy-Plug wiring system.
